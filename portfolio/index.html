WebInteractive web lessonSpring 2026Design lead

Thought Check

A ten-minute web lesson that teaches people to notice three cognitive distortions: all-or-nothing thinking, catastrophizing, and mind reading.

The flow

Learn each distortion with a real example and a balanced reframe, practice spotting it with a reveal, then take a short quiz. A progress bar and a sidebar show where you are at every step.

What I designed

  • The page flow and every screen, from the home page to the results recap
  • A small design system: four core colors, an 8px spacing scale, and exactly five type sizes
  • Quiz feedback that explains why the answer you picked does not fit, instead of only marking it wrong

Course project, User Interface Design, Columbia University. Built in Flask, HTML/CSS, and Bootstrap 5 with Silin Jiang, who led development.

Client Outreach Templates

Cold email and LinkedIn templates for an HR and leadership consulting firm. I produced the whole deliverable, from choosing which client segments to target to the finished template pages.

What I made

  • An assessment of eight professional-services segments that narrowed the outreach to four
  • A cold email for each of the four segments, with three subject-line options per email
  • A short LinkedIn message for each segment
  • The finished template pages, set in the client's existing brand

Student consulting engagement as Senior Marketing Consultant. To protect the client, its name, contact details, logo, and segment analysis are withheld. The brand styling on these pages is the client's, not my work.
